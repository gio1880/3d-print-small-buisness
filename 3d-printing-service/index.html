Send a file, or just an idea

Already have a model? If you are thinking 'can someone 3D print my file', the answer is yes: send an STL or 3MF and tell us how many you need and what the part is for. No file? That is fine too. Send a photo, a sketch or a short description, and we will tell you in the quote what it takes to make it.

For a part that has to fit something, measurements matter more than anything. A photo with a ruler next to the part, plus the key sizes, saves a lot of back and forth.

What prints well in PLA

We print in PLA, a rigid plastic that holds fine detail and clean edges. It is a great match for:

  • Brackets, clips, knobs, cable holders and shelf pegs
  • Desk and drawer organizers
  • Prototype housings and test fits
  • Classroom models, game pieces and figurines
  • Name tags, keychains, medals and party favors

PLA is not the right choice for everything. It softens with heat, so skip it for parts that live in a hot car, near a stove or in a dishwasher. It is also not meant for parts under heavy constant load or for food and drink use. Tell us where the part will live and we will be straight with you about whether PLA fits.

Sizes and fit

Our printers are desktop machines, so the sweet spot is parts that fit in your hands, from small clips up to toy-sized figures. Larger pieces can often be printed in sections and joined, and we will tell you in the quote if that is needed.

Not sure how big your part is? Measure the longest side and include it with your request, and we will tell you whether it prints in one piece.

For snap fits and mating parts, plan on a test print. A first piece confirms the fit before you order more.

Replacement parts and prototypes

3D printed replacement parts are often the fastest fix for a broken battery cover, a missing knob or a cracked organizer clip, especially when the original part is no longer sold. For a prototype, PLA is a quick, low-cost way to check size, shape and feel before you invest in anything bigger.

Students and teachers use the same approach for science fair builds and class projects, where a printed model is often the piece that makes the idea click.

One-off prints, replacement parts and prototypes are priced by job, so ask for a quote. If the design works and you need more, small batch 3D printing of the same part is next, and reorders of the same design are 10% off.

Questions people ask

Which file types do you accept?

Send an STL or 3MF file. If you do not have a file, a photo, sketch or description with measurements works as a starting point.

Can you print in ABS, PETG, nylon or resin?

We print in PLA. If your part needs something PLA cannot handle, like high heat, we will tell you up front rather than print something that fails.

Will a PLA part survive in my car?

Probably not in summer. PLA softens with heat, so it is a poor fit for car interiors, dishwashers or anything near a stove.

How much does a one-off 3D print cost?

One-off prints, replacement parts and prototypes are priced by job. Send your file or idea and ask for a quote.

I do not know the exact size. Can you still help?

Send a photo with a ruler or tape measure next to the part and a few key measurements. That is usually enough to start a quote.
