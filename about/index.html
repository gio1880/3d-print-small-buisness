About

A small maker shop in Queens and Westchester

GlitchPrints designs and makes custom pieces for local families, schools, teams, restaurants and events, on our own 3D printers and laser cutter.

Who we are

The shop

  • 3D printers: a row of Bambu Lab A1s, a Bambu Lab P1S and an Anycubic, printing in PLA.
  • Laser cutter and engraver: for birch plywood, coasters and cast acrylic.
  • Design: every order is drawn up in-house and sent to you as a proof first.

Because everything is made here, we can mix printed and laser cut parts in one order, fix a typo the same day and reprint a single piece if one goes missing.
