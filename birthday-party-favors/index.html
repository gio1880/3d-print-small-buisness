The $69 birthday party pack

The simplest way to handle favors is the birthday party pack. You get 12 favors, each with a guest's name on it, plus a custom acrylic cake topper for the birthday kid. All for $69.

Choose named fidget clickers or flexi dragons and animals for the favors. Send us the guest list, the birthday kid's name and age for the topper, and the party date. That one order covers the favor bags and the cake. If you only need the topper, see our custom cake toppers page.

Clickers or flexi dragons: which fits your party?

Both are 3D printed party favors made from PLA, and both are easy for kids to carry home. They just suit different parties.

  • Name clickers are fidget keychains with a button that clicks. Every guest's name is on their own clicker, so nobody fights over whose is whose. See more on our custom clicker keychains page.
  • Flexi dragons and animals have bendy, segmented bodies that wiggle when you move them. They fit dragon, dinosaur and animal themes. Adding a name costs $0.50 each.

Flexi dragon party favors come 10 for $25, 25 for $50 or 50 for $90, so they're the lower cost choice for a big guest list. Clickers cost a bit more but include the name. Can't decide? Ask for a free proof of each and pick from there.

Personalized party favors for kids in your theme

Want something that matches the party exactly? We can design a custom favor toy in your theme, like a mascot or a logo shape. Think a rocket for a space party, a soccer ball for a team party, or the birthday kid's favorite animal.

Custom theme favors have a $25 design fee, then cost $3 each at 25 or $2.50 each at 50. They take 5 to 8 days. Add laser cut wood favor tags to the goodie bags for $3 each at 10, $2.50 at 25 or $2 at 50. Names on tags are free.

Personalized party favors for kids work best when they fit the party, so tell us the theme, the colors and the age group when you ask for a quote.

Plan your timing

Here's how long each part takes once you approve the proof:

  • Name clickers and flexi favors: 4 to 7 days
  • Custom theme favors: 5 to 8 days
  • Cake topper and wood favor tags: 2 to 4 days

For a party pack, plan on about a week. If the party is sooner, rush orders under 3 days cost 25% more. Favor orders start at 10 pieces. One heads up for parties with little siblings: clickers and small favors have small parts and are not for children under 3.

Tip: send the guest list as soon as RSVPs come in, and add a few spare names for siblings or last-minute guests. That's easier than a second rush order.

Questions people ask

What comes in the $69 birthday party pack?

Twelve favors with a guest's name on each one, either name clickers or flexi favors, plus one custom acrylic cake topper. If your guest list is bigger than 12, ask for a quote.

Can I order fewer than 10 favors?

Favor orders start at 10 pieces. The cake topper is the exception and can be ordered on its own.

Do flexi dragons come with names?

They can. Adding a name to a flexi favor costs $0.50 each, while names on clickers are free. Favors in the party pack already come named.

Are these favors OK for toddlers?

No. Clickers and small favors have small parts and are not for children under 3, so keep them out of the youngest guests' bags.

The party is in two days. Can you still make favors?

Rush orders under 3 days cost 25% more. Message us with your date and quantity and we'll tell you what can be ready in time.
