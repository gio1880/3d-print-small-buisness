Personalized Christmas ornaments in wood or acrylic

We laser cut custom ornaments from wood or acrylic with names, a year, a family name or a short message. They make good first Christmas keepsakes, teacher gifts and matching sets for a whole family.

Some ideas:

  • A baby's first Christmas ornament with the name and year
  • A matching set with each family member's name
  • Teacher or coach ornaments with a short thank you
  • Company ornaments with your logo for clients and staff

Because we make them locally, personalized ornaments for NYC and Westchester families don't wait on cross-country shipping. Ornaments aren't priced yet, so ask for a quote. Tell us how many you need, what text goes on each one, and whether you want wood or acrylic. Ornaments are laser items, so the Christmas order-by date is Dec 16.

Christmas clickers: five holiday shapes

Our Christmas name clickers are 3D printed fidget keychains in five shapes: tree, snowman, ornament, gingerbread and candy cane. Each one gets a name, and kids can click them all day.

  • 3-pack, $12: easy stocking stuffers for siblings or cousins.
  • 6-pack, $21: covers a bigger family or a group of friends.

For everyday clickers and bulk pricing outside the holidays, see custom clicker keychains.

Custom Christmas gifts don't have to be big. A named clicker in a stocking or tied to a present gets opened first and clicked all morning.

Class Christmas gifts and team gifts

The class or team 25-pack gives every kid a named Christmas clicker with a wood gift tag for $95. That's $3.80 per kid, and it's ready to hand out before winter break. Coaches can use the same pack for a holiday team party.
