The budget pick for bulk personalized keychains

If you need a lot of keychains and every dollar counts, wood is the way to go. At 50 pieces they're $2.50 each, the lowest per-piece price on our menu. Names are free, so a list of 50 different names costs the same as 50 of one name.

They're also fast. Laser cutting is quicker than 3D printing, so wood keychains are ready in 2 to 4 days after you approve your proof. Each one is cut from 1/8 in birch plywood, which keeps them light and shows off the wood grain around the engraving.

Engraved wood keychains have a warm, natural look without any paint, so they fit almost any brand or event color scheme.

Logo keychains for businesses

Wood logo keychains make a simple giveaway for a shop, studio, gym or restaurant. Hand them to new customers, put a bowl on the counter or include them in a welcome kit for staff.

A custom logo has a one-time $25 setup fee. After that, the design is saved and reused free on every reorder, and reorders of the same design get 10% off. So the second batch costs less than the first. Want more engraved pieces with your logo? See our laser engraving service.

Wood name keychains for schools and events

Keychains for events are easy to hand out, since every guest can find their own name on the table. Some ways people use wood name keychains:

  • Backpack tags for a new class or a field trip group
  • Favors at a family reunion, shower or wedding, with each guest's name
  • Name tags for a club, conference or volunteer day
  • Thank you gifts for coaches and helpers

For weddings and parties, they pair well with matching table numbers and place cards. For sports teams, our team pack puts a medal and a name keychain in every player's hand for $10 each. See custom medals.

Wood keychain or clicker keychain?

We make two kinds of name keychains, and they suit different jobs:

  • Wood keychains: flat, light and engraved. $4 to $2.50 each. Ready in 2 to 4 days. Best for businesses, events and tight budgets.
  • Clicker keychains: 3D printed with a button that clicks. $5 to $3.50 each. Ready in 4 to 7 days. Best for kids who want a fidget toy.

If the keychains are for a group of kids, see our custom clicker keychains.
