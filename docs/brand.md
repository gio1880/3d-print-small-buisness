# Brand rules

## Name

**GlitchPrints**, one word, capital G and P. The wordmark shows "Glitch" in ink and
"Prints" in magenta. GlitchPrints is its own brand; don't mix it with other programs.

## Colors

| Role | Light mode | Dark mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cyan (main) | `#00b4d6` (text on light: `#006f86`) | `#33d4f2` |
| Magenta (main, buttons and actions) | `#d4136f` | `#ff4f9f` |
| Ink (text) | `#14161f` | `#e9eef5` |
| Paper (page background) | `#f6f8fb` | `#0e1117` |

- Cyan and magenta are the main colors. Lime is allowed as a small accent only.
- Business pages (schools, restaurants, parties) use light backgrounds.
- Colors are CSS variables at the top of each page's `<style>`; change them there.

## Fonts

- **Orbitron** for headings only (`h1` to `h3`, wordmark, big numbers).
- **Inter** for body text.
- Both load from Google Fonts.

## Voice

- Plain, friendly and specific: prices, turnaround and pickup up front.
- Talk to the buyer ("you"), short sentences, no jargon.
- Honest: real photos, real reviews, no claims we can't back up.
