# Business overview

## What GlitchPrints is

A small local maker shop that designs and produces custom 3D printed and laser cut
pieces in batches. The pitch: free design and a free digital proof, made locally on our
own machines, ready in days, free local pickup, no shipping wait.

## Who buys

| Group | What they order | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Schools, PTAs, camps and teams | 25 to 50 named favors, class gifts, medals | Biggest orders |
| Restaurants and cafés | Table numbers, QR code stands, review stands, logo coasters, signs | Few local makers target them |
| Families | Birthday favor packs, cake toppers, toys made from a kid's drawing, holiday gifts | Smaller, frequent orders |
| Weddings and events | Table numbers, place cards, coasters, cake toppers | Seasonal |
| Makers and small businesses | One-off 3D prints and laser engraving from their own files | Quoted per job |

Etsy is a small side channel at most; the site is built for local customers.

## How ordering works

1. The customer sends the quote form on `/contact/`, an email or a text.
2. GlitchPrints replies, usually within a day, with a price and a digital proof.
3. The order is made on GlitchPrints' own machines.
4. Free pickup in Flushing or Hartsdale, $10 local delivery, or shipping (quoted).

Order rules are in [pricing.md](pricing.md).

## Equipment (production capacity)

- Several 3D printers: Bambu Lab A1s and a P1S, plus an Anycubic (model unconfirmed).
- One laser cutter and engraver (model and bed size unconfirmed).

The laser does every name tag, coaster, table number and sign, so laser work is the
usual bottleneck. Turnaround on the site: laser items 2 to 4 days, 3D printed items
4 to 7 days, toys from a drawing 7 to 10 days. Rush under 3 days adds 25%.
