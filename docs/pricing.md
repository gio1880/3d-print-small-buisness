## Order rules

- Minimum 10 pieces, except cake toppers and toys from a drawing.
- Names, ages and table numbers are free to customize.
- Custom logo or new design: $25 one-time setup, reused free on reorders.
- Rush under 3 days: add 25%.
- Reorders of the same design: 10% off.
- Pickup free in Flushing and Hartsdale; local delivery $10; shipping quoted.
