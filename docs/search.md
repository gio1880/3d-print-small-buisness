## What we learned

- Local results are mostly directories and online services. No local gift maker ranked
  for Queens or Westchester searches at the time of research, which is the opening.
- Seasonal peaks: ornaments in November and December, teacher gifts in May and June,
  party favors year round.
- A Google Business Profile matters a lot for "near me" searches (inferred; the map
  results weren't visible to the research tool).

## When writing or editing a page

- Use the target search in the `<title>`, the `<h1>` and the first paragraph, naturally.
- Mention the area (Queens, Westchester, NYC) where it reads naturally.
- Keep one page per search; don't make two pages compete for the same one.
- Answer real buyer questions in the FAQ (price, minimum, turnaround, pickup).
