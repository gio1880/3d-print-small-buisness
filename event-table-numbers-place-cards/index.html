One look across every table

Wedding table numbers and place cards look best when they clearly belong together. We cut both from the same 1/8 in birch plywood, so the color and grain match from the welcome table to the last seat. Add wood favor tags and the whole room feels planned.

Planning a smaller gathering? The 10 piece minimum works for an intimate dinner, and prices drop as your guest list grows.

Names and table numbers are free to customize. If you want your monogram, crest or event logo on every piece, that is a one-time $25 design setup.

Place cards with every guest name

Our laser cut place cards are $3 each at 10, $2.50 at 25 and $2 at 50, with the guest name engraved on each one. There is no per-name charge.

  • Send a typed list. One name per line, spelled exactly as it should appear.
  • Group by table. If the cards also show a table number, put that next to each name.
  • Check the proof. Your free digital proof shows every card, so you can catch a misspelled name before it is engraved.

The same pieces work as escort cards at the entrance or as tags on favor bags.

Event table numbers for the reception

Our event table numbers are freestanding wood pieces that need no holder, priced the same as our restaurant numbers: $5 each at 10, $4 at 25 and $3.50 at 50. Number them 1 to 20, or swap numbers for table names, such as favorite cities or song titles, at no extra cost.

Pair the numbers with a seating chart at the entrance so guests find their table fast.

Prefer acrylic table numbers for a modern look? Acrylic versions are made to order, so ask for a quote.

Ideas for showers, mitzvahs, sweet 16s and communions

  • Baby and bridal showers: wood favor tags tied to jars of treats or small plants, with the guest of honor's name and date.
  • Bar and bat mitzvahs: bar mitzvah table numbers named after the teen's interests, with matching place cards.
  • Sweet 16: sweet 16 place cards with each friend's name, paired with an acrylic cake topper for $25.
  • First communions: favor tags with the child's name and the date for each family to take home.

Want something guests keep? Add engraved coasters at each seat.

Questions people ask

When should I order place cards?

Order right after RSVPs close so your guest list is final. They are ready 2 to 4 days after proof approval, and rush is available for 25% more.

Do you charge extra for each guest name?

No. Names and table numbers are free to customize. Only a custom monogram or new design has a one-time $25 setup.

What if a guest is added after I order?

Order a couple of extra cards up front to be safe. Reorders of the same design are 10% off, with our standard 10 piece minimum.

Can I get acrylic table numbers instead of wood?

Yes, acrylic event table numbers are made to order. Ask for a quote with your table count and date.

Can the table numbers be names instead of numbers?

Yes. Send the table names you want, like cities or song titles, and they will appear on your free proof.
