Meet the Team

Support Us →
Premium Rice Cookers · FTC Robotics

We're Premium Rice Cookers, a student-run FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) robotics team based in Flushing, Queens, New York City. Every season we design, build, wire, and program a competition robot from the ground up — then take it head-to-head against teams from across the region.

Selling our 3D prints is how we fund the season, and it's also how we learn — real engineering, CAD, coding, and the business side of running a team. Every print you buy and every dollar you donate keeps students on the field, building and competing.

FAQ

Send the idea, size, color, quantity, and any reference files. You will get a quote before anything is printed.

Most small prints are ready within 48 hours once the design and color are confirmed.

Every letter is its own clicky button. Pick a layout — a straight row (2–12 buttons), a 2×2 grid (4 buttons), or a 3×3 grid (9 buttons) — then type your letters, choose a color, and add any add-ons. The length is set automatically by how many buttons you use: Mini is 2–4, Standard is 5–8, Long is 9–12. The price updates live as you build, and the exact text, layout, color, and add-ons come through with your order.

Event packages are priced per person, and everyone gets their own clicker with their own name on it. Small events (5–10 people) are $12 each, medium events (11–25) are $10 each, and large events (26+) are $8 each — so a 30-person team is $240. Send us your headcount and date through the quote form, then a list of names once you have it. Events are quoted rather than checked out online, because we confirm your name list and timeline first.

We're a student robotics team building these by hand between school and practice, so the 10-per-person cap keeps turnaround times honest for everyone. If you need a class set, team order, or event favors beyond 10, use the custom request form and we'll quote a bulk order with a proper timeline.

A theme pack ($6) restyles your clicker around a subject — sports, gaming, holiday, school spirit, healthcare, or music — with themed button faces and a matching color scheme. Tell us the theme in the notes at checkout, or send a reference image through the custom request form.
