Materials we cut and engrave

We keep to a short list of materials so results are predictable:

  • 1/8 in birch plywood for keychains, place cards, table numbers, menu holders and tags. Wood engraving burns the design into the surface for a warm, darker mark.
  • 1/4 in plywood for coasters and pieces that need more heft.
  • Cast acrylic for cake toppers, QR stands and signs. Acrylic engraving leaves a frosted look that stands out nicely on clear material.

Sticking to these lets us quote quickly and tell you exactly how a finished piece will look.

Logo engraving that comes out sharp

Logo engraving is only as clean as the artwork you send. A vector file such as SVG or PDF gives the crispest edges. A large, clear PNG also works. Very thin lines and tiny text can blur at small sizes, so we may suggest a simpler version for small pieces like keychains.

A custom logo or new design is a one-time $25 setup. We keep it on file and reuse it free on reorders, and reorders of the same design are 10% off. You approve a free digital proof before anything is cut.

Planning a laser project

A little planning makes laser work faster and cheaper:

  • Pick the material first. Wood feels warm and handmade. Acrylic looks clean and modern, and suits signs and cake toppers.
  • Think in one color. Engraving is a single tone, so logos with gradients or photos need a simplified version.
  • Tell us the use. A sign for a busy counter and a keepsake for a shelf may call for a different size or thickness.
  • Batch it. Prices drop at 25 and 50 pieces, so grouping orders for a school year or a season saves money.

Questions people ask

What file should I send for logo engraving?

A vector file like SVG or PDF is best. A large, clear PNG also works, and you will see the result on a free proof.

Can you engrave metal, glass or leather?

We work in birch plywood and cast acrylic. If your project needs another material, we are not the right shop for it.

How much does a custom acrylic sign cost?

Custom acrylic signs are priced per job. Ask for a quote with the size, wording and how many you need.

Is there a minimum order for laser items?

Yes, 10 pieces for most items. Acrylic cake toppers are the exception at $25 for one.

What does engraving look like on clear acrylic?

It leaves a frosted mark that shows up well against the clear material, especially with light behind it.
