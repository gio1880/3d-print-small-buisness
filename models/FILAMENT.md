# Filament: which colours each model needs

All PLA. Grams are Bambu Studio's estimate on the A1 for **one piece alone on the plate**, purge included, split by slot. On a full plate the purge is shared, so each piece uses much less.

The colour named below is what each project is set to. Any spool of a similar colour works: put it in that slot and change the slot colour in the slicer so the preview matches.

## Spools to keep stocked

| Spool | Used by |

## By printer

All the projects use slot numbers, so load the spools the same way on every machine.

| Printer | Slots | Big-eye animals | Two-colour pieces |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bambu A1 (home or school) | AMS Lite, 4 | 1 body · 2 white · 3 black | 1 body · 2 accent |
| Bambu P1S (home) | AMS, 4 | same as A1 | same as A1 |
| Anycubic Kobra S1 (home) | multi-colour unit | load the three STLs as one object, then give each part its colour: body / white / black | load the two STLs, body / accent |
| School A1 × 4 | AMS Lite, 4 | same as A1 (you pay the filament: log it in `STOCK.md`) | same |

**Tip:** keep white and black loaded in slots 2 and 3 on one Bambu printer for the big-eye animals, and change only slot 1 between animals. The panda is the exception: white goes in slot 1 and slot 2 stays empty.
