You bring the QR code, we build the stand

A QR code table stand only works if it points to the right place. That is why we build each stand around the code you already use. Send us the QR code image from your menu, ordering or POS provider, plus your logo, and we lay out a clean acrylic QR menu stand with both.

Common codes we see on tables and counters:

  • A digital menu link
  • Online ordering or order-at-table
  • A QR code for Wi-Fi, so guests stop asking for the password
  • A payment or tipping link
  • Your Instagram or a loyalty sign-up page

How to make sure it scans

A good QR code sign for restaurant tables is easy to scan from a seat, in low light, on any phone. A few rules help:

  • High contrast. Dark code on a light background scans best. Avoid pale colors or busy patterns behind the code.
  • Do not shrink it. Bigger is easier, especially for guests at the far side of the table.
  • Leave a margin. Keep blank space around the code. Text or a logo pressed up against it can confuse cameras.
  • Skip logo overlays. Do not paste a logo on top of the code unless your QR generator built it in.
  • Test before you approve. Scan the free digital proof with an iPhone and an Android phone, in the lighting of your own room.

Use a code that can change later

Menus change, ordering platforms change and Wi-Fi passwords change. If your QR provider offers a dynamic code, one that redirects to a link you can edit, use it. Then your stands keep working when the link behind them moves, and you never have to replace a stand because of a URL.

If you do need new stands later, reorders of the same design are 10% off and your logo setup is reused free.
